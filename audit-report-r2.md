## What is checked

- **Every follow-up beat and prompt** goes through the same fact extraction as a main answer — numbers with units, section references, identifiers, steps — and each fact must be found, verbatim or trivially reformatted, in the chain: the follow-up's `source` (a heading path, bold label, table or list number, or a whole document: `story-scripts.md`, `study-guide.md`, `round1-debrief.md`) → the experience sources for own-it / drill layers → the card's own section → the files → both files. Not found anywhere is a finding; found only outside the named source is a review note (as for scenario beats).
- **own-it and drill beats** must trace (≥ 50% of their content words on one line) to `story-scripts.md` (its "Open questions" list excluded), the guide's Interview playbook, or the debrief's "Facts about my own work" list. A beat that does not is accepted only as the common block's disclaimer ("say plainly there's no direct story…") or as a "how I'd run it" procedure that sits in the guide and does not read as first-person experience; anything else is a finding. Every bridge is listed in the appendix for the reading pass.
- **find-out** layers only on `confirmInEdition: true` cards; a `ladder` card's layers are all drills.
- **Bracketed items**: a graded beat may not carry `[…]` and may not restate a bracketed (open) line of the sources (≥ 60% of its content words in one bracket). Fill-in beats (`{ "label", "fillIn": true }`) are open items — shown, never graded — and are not checked as fact.
- **Prompts**: no multiple choice (option markers, "which of the following", yes/no…); a `number` follow-up's prompt may not contain every number its beats teach.
- **Walkthrough cards**: the beats' positions in the card's section are monotone (the guide's steps in order).
- **Mock decks with a round**: each follow-up layer with beats is checked like a card follow-up, scoped to the question's section plus every section its topic's cards cite plus, when the question copies a card (`cardId`, decision 84), that card's follow-up sources; own-it / drill layers add the experience sources. A layer beat that is not a verbatim copy of the linked card's is noted.
- **Coverage targets** (session B common block): every Core scenario card (walkthroughs aside) has 2 follow-ups with a why-not or what-if; every Core topic has ≥ 2 scenario cards with follow-ups; every Core `confirmInEdition` scenario has a find-out; every technical area has an own-it; every Support topic has a scenario card with 1–2 follow-ups; Light topics unchanged; walkthroughs are scenario cards at par 120 with 1–2 follow-ups. Session C: the I3–I7 story cards carry drill follow-ups, the two ladders have five drill layers, I8 is untouched.
- **Not mechanical**: whether a beat that traces to a story line says something the story does not (a true clause with an invented tail), and whether a guide-procedure bridge is the right procedure. Those are the reading pass; the appendix lists every candidate.
